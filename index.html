Double-A Manager Interview Exercise

Objective

Demonstrate how you would organize and execute baseball activities throughout a typical month at the Double-A level.

1Build a Monthly PlanUse the Monthly Planner to organize the baseball activities, team priorities, and developmental work you believe should occur throughout a month.
2Create Two Daily SchedulesSelect any two days from your monthly calendar and construct detailed daily practice schedules.
3Save and SubmitExport your completed exercise so it can be reviewed during the interview process.

A few notes

  • There is no prescribed planning methodology.
  • You may use suggested baseball activities or create your own.
  • You have complete flexibility in how you organize your month and daily schedules.
  • Your work saves automatically in your browser, but you should export a backup before leaving or switching devices.
