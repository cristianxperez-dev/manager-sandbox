Manager Interview Sandbox

Use this workspace to show how you would evaluate players, plan their development, and organize the baseball activities of a Double-A club.

Review four player cases and write an evaluation and a development prescription for each. Then build a monthly plan that reflects your priorities, and select two dates to construct detailed daily practice schedules.

There is no prescribed method or format, and you can move between sections and revise at any time. We are interested in your priorities, your organizational approach, and how you translate your philosophy into practical work.

3Plan the monthOrganize a month of activities however you think about it.
4Build two daysDetailed practice schedules for two dates.
